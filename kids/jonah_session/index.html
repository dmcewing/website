    Knight Force Ministry Curriculum

    Session: The Quest to the Deep (The Story of Jonah)

    Target Audience: Primary School Children (Ages 5 to 10)
    Theme: Hearing God's Voice, Obedience, and His Incredible Mercy
    Key Verse:

    "Is there any place I can go to get away from your Spirit? Where can I run from you?" — Psalm 139:7 (NIRV)

    Objectives

    By the end of this session, children will understand:

    1. You cannot hide from God: God knows where we are, what we are thinking, and He cares about our obedience.
    2. The Voice of the Spirit: The Holy Spirit guides us and prompts us to obey God's commands.
    3. God's Great Mercy: When we make mistakes or run away, God is quick to forgive us when we repent (turn around).
    4. Love for Others: God's mercy is for everyone, even people who have been unkind or are hard to love.

    Session Outline (approx. 70 minutes)

    Section Duration Leader Responsible
    1. The Gathering & Icebreaker 10 mins Mia (Lady Kindness) & Leo (Sir Braveheart)
    2. Praise & Worship 10 mins Ben (Sir Joyful)
    3. Skit: The Royal Decree 10 mins Entire Knight Force Cast
    4. Interactive Story: Jonah's Run & Rescue 15 mins Ben (Sir Truthful) & Cast
    5. Small Group Application 15 mins Small Group Leaders (Chloe & Mia)
    6. Holy Spirit Ministry Time 10 mins Chloe (Lady Faith) & Leaders

    1. The Gathering & Icebreaker (10 mins)

    Activity: "The King Says: Run or Follow"

    • Led by: Leo (Sir Braveheart) and Mia (Lady Kindness)
    • Setup: Clear the center of the room. Draw a line in the middle of the floor using masking tape. One side is labeled "Tarshish" (Running Away) and the other side is "Nineveh" (Obeying).

    • How to Play:

    • Leo explains: "Knights, when the King gives an order, a true knight obeys immediately! In this game, I will call out different instructions. If I call out a command from the King (e.g., 'Feed the hungry,' 'Show kindness,' 'Tell the truth'), you must quickly jump to the Nineveh side and shout 'Obey!'"

    • "But if I call out a command that represents running away or doing your own thing (e.g., 'Hide under the bed,' 'Ignore the voice,' 'Run to the ship'), you must jump to the Tarshish side and freeze!"
    • Mia adds: "Sometimes we want to run away when things get hard, but today we’re going to learn why following God's voice is always the best path. Let's practice listening closely!"
    • Play a few rounds, speeding up the commands to test their listening skills.

    2. Praise & Worship (10 mins)

    Led by: Ben (Sir Joyful)

    • Focus: Transitioning from high energy to a place of praise and sensitivity to the Holy Spirit.
    • Song 1 (Upbeat): "Every Move I Make" or "I Will Follow" (Celebrating that we move and live in God's presence).
    • Sir Joyful's Tip: "Get your shields up and your dancing feet ready! We praise a God who is with us wherever we go—even in the deepest ocean!"
    • Song 2 (Reflective): "Holy Spirit, You Are Welcome Here" or "Create in Me a Clean Heart" (Preparing hearts to listen to the promptings of the Spirit).
    • Sir Joyful's Tip: "Let’s quiet our hearts. Raise your hands if you want to. Let's ask the Holy Spirit to speak to us today."

    3. Skit: The Royal Decree (10 mins)

    Characters: Leo (Sir Braveheart), Mia (Lady Kindness), Ben (Sir Truthful), Chloe (Lady Faith), Ben (Sir Joyful)

    (The scene opens with the Knight Force gathered around a table. Ben/Sir Joyful is humming and tuning his instrument. Leo is practicing his sword swings. Chloe is looking at a map. Mia is organizing bandages.)

    Leo: (Swinging sword) Hah! Yah! Bring on the dragons! Knight Force is ready for any battle the King throws our way!

    Chloe: (Looking up) Leo, the King just sent a message. It's a special royal scroll, delivered by the royal messenger.

    Ben (Sir Truthful): (Holding up a scroll) And according to the Royal Code, Chapter 1, Section 3, all decrees must be read immediately and followed with absolute integrity. Let’s see what our mission is.

    Mia: (Gently) I hope it’s a mission to help the villagers in the valley. They've been having a tough harvest.

    Ben (Sir Truthful): (Opens scroll, reads) "To the Knight Force: Go at once to the camp of the Shadow Knights. You are to deliver a package of healing medicine, fresh bread, and an invitation to the King’s Grand Feast."

    (Silence falls over the team. Leo stops swinging his sword. Sir Joyful stops playing.)

    Leo: (Incredulous) The... Shadow Knights? The ones who threw mud at our armor last week? The ones who keep trying to steal our training shields? King, you must be joking! We should be marching there to fight them, not feed them!

    Ben (Sir Joyful): (Frowning) Yeah, playing a victory song for those guys doesn't sound very fun. They don't even like music.

    Mia: But Leo, the King says they are hurting. Some of them are sick, and they don't have enough food. This is our chance to show them the King's kindness.

    Leo: No way. I’m not doing it. In fact... (looks at the map) look! The port of Tarshish is only a day’s ride away. There’s a ship leaving today. Let's just go on a nice, peaceful cruise instead. We can tell the King we... got lost!

    Chloe: (Stepping in front of Leo) Leo! You’re Sir Braveheart. You always take the first step. But running away from a mission isn't brave. And we can't hide from the King. He sees everything.

    Ben (Sir Truthful): Chloe is right, Leo. You can't hide from the truth. In fact, this reminds me exactly of a famous story from our Royal Archives. A story about a prophet named Jonah who tried to do the exact same thing.

    Ben (Sir Joyful): (Excitedly) Ooh! The one with the giant fish? I love that story! Let's tell it!

    4. Interactive Story: Jonah's Run & Rescue (15 mins)

    Led by: Ben (Sir Truthful) with the rest of the cast acting out parts and leading the kids in interactive actions.

    • Narrator (Sir Truthful): "Long ago, God spoke to a prophet named Jonah. God said, 'Go to the great city of Nineveh and preach against it, because their wickedness has come up before me.' Now, Nineveh was a mean place. The people there were enemies of Israel. Jonah didn't want to go. He thought they deserved to be punished, not saved."
    • Mia: "So, Jonah decided to run away. He went down to the port of Joppa, paid the fare, and got on a boat headed for Tarshish—the exact opposite direction!"

    • [KIDS INTERACTION]: Mia tells the kids: "Let's all pretend we are rowing the boat to Tarshish. Row! Row! Row! We think we are safe!"

    • Narrator (Sir Truthful): "But you cannot run away from God. God sent a great wind on the sea, and such a violent storm arose that the ship threatened to break apart."

    • Leo: "The storm was fierce! The wind howled!"
    • [KIDS INTERACTION]: Leo leads the kids to make howling wind sounds: "Whooooosh! Whooooosh!"
    • Chloe: "The waves crashed against the sides!"

    • [KIDS INTERACTION]: Chloe leads the kids to sway side to side like they are in a rocking boat.

    • Narrator (Sir Truthful): "The sailors were terrified! They started throwing their cargo into the sea to lighten the ship. But where was Jonah? Fast asleep in the bottom of the boat! The captain woke him up and said, 'How can you sleep? Call on your God!'"

    • Sir Joyful: "The sailors realized the storm was because Jonah was running away from the Lord. Jonah told them, 'Pick me up and throw me into the sea, and it will become calm. I know that it is my fault that this great storm has come upon you.'"
    • Leo: "So, the sailors picked Jonah up and... SPLASH! They threw him into the sea!"

    • [KIDS INTERACTION]: Have all the kids shout "SPLASH!" and immediately stop making storm noises. Complete silence.

    • Narrator (Sir Truthful): "As soon as Jonah hit the water, the sea became completely calm. But Jonah was sinking down, down, down into the deep, dark water. Seaweed wrapped around his head. He thought he was going to drown. But God had a plan. God provided a huge fish—a giant whale—to swallow Jonah!"

    • [PROP ACTION]: A large cardboard box decorated like a fish mouth or a pop-up tunnel is shown. A leader representing Jonah crawls inside.
    • Mia: "Jonah was inside that dark, smelly fish belly for three days and three nights. That gave him a lot of time to think. And in the dark, Jonah prayed to God. He repented. He said, 'God, I was wrong. I will do what you asked. Salvation comes from the Lord!'"

    • [KIDS INTERACTION]: Mia asks the kids to whisper a quiet prayer: "God, forgive me when I disobey. Help me to listen to your voice."

    • Narrator (Sir Truthful): "God heard Jonah's prayer. He commanded the giant fish, and..."

    • Sir Joyful: "BLETCH! The fish spit Jonah out onto dry land!"

    • [KIDS INTERACTION]: Everyone makes a funny spitting sound: "Ptooey!"

    • Narrator (Sir Truthful): "God gave Jonah a second chance. He said again, 'Go to Nineveh.' This time, Jonah obeyed. He walked through the city and told them God's message. And guess what? The people of Nineveh listened! They repented, they said sorry to God, and God showed them mercy and did not destroy them."

    (Return to the Knight Force characters)

    Leo: (Thinking deeply) Wow. Jonah tried to run, but God's love was bigger than his running. And God cared about the people of Nineveh, even though they were mean.

    Mia: Just like the King cares about the Shadow Knights.

    Leo: (Nods, putting sword away) You're right. I don't want to end up inside a giant fish! And more than that, I want to obey our King. The Holy Spirit is prompting my heart right now—I need to let go of my anger and go help them.

    Chloe: That's the spirit, Sir Braveheart! Let's trust God and take the package to the Shadow Knights together.

    Ben (Sir Joyful): And I’ll play a song of peace all the way there! Let's praise God because He always gives us a second chance!

    5. Small Group Application (15 mins)

    • Break the children into two age groups (Ages 5-7 and Ages 8-10) for discussion and application.*

    Group A: Junior Knights (Ages 5-7)

    • Goal: Understand the story basics, that God is always with us, and that we must obey.

    • Discussion Questions:

    • Why did Jonah run away from God? (He was scared, he didn't like the people of Nineveh).

    • Can you ever hide from God? What does our memory verse say? (No, God is always with us!).
    • How did God show Jonah mercy? (He sent a big fish to save him from drowning, and gave him a second chance).
    • What is one thing your parents or God asks you to do that is hard to obey? How can we ask the Holy Spirit to help us?
    • Quick Activity: Jonah in the Fish Cup. Give kids a paper cup (the fish) and a small paper cutout of Jonah on a string. They can drop Jonah into the cup and pull him out as they retell the story.

    Group B: Senior Knights (Ages 8-10)

    • Goal: Explore promptings of the Holy Spirit, dealing with difficult people, and true repentance.

    • Discussion Questions:

    • Jonah didn't want the Ninevites to receive mercy because they were enemies. Have you ever felt like someone who was mean to you didn't deserve kindness? How does God want us to treat them?

    • Read Psalm 139:7-10. How does it feel to know that no matter where you go, God's Spirit is there? Is it comforting or scary? (Comforting, because we are never alone; it also reminds us we can't hide our choices from Him).
    • When Jonah was in the fish, he prayed a prayer of thanksgiving before he was even spit out. Why is it important to praise God even when we are in a "dark place"?
    • How does the Holy Spirit speak to us today? How do we know when He is prompting us to obey? (A quiet voice in our heart, a feeling that we need to do what is right, matching what the Bible says).

    6. Holy Spirit Ministry Time (10 mins)

    • Led by: Chloe (Lady Faith) and Mia (Lady Kindness)
    • Atmosphere: Soft instrumental worship music playing (Sir Joyful playing acoustic guitar or keyboard track).

    Leader Script & Prayer Steps:

    1. The Invitation: "Kids, Jonah tried to run away from God's presence, but God's Holy Spirit followed him to the bottom of the sea. Today, God's Spirit is right here in this room. You don't have to run from Him. He loves you so much."
    2. Quiet Reflection: "Let's all close our eyes and place our hands on our hearts. Let's ask the Holy Spirit a question. Silence your mind and listen: 'Holy Spirit, is there any area in my life where I have been running away from what You want me to do?'" (Pause for 30 seconds of quiet).
    3. Repentance & Cleansing: "If the Holy Spirit showed you something—maybe disobeying your parents, holding onto anger, or hiding a lie—just talk to Him in your heart right now. Say, 'Jesus, I am sorry. Thank You for Your mercy. Clean my heart and give me a second chance.'"
    4. Empowerment to Obey: "Now, let's ask the Holy Spirit to fill us with His power. In the Pentecostal church, we believe the Holy Spirit gives us the power to be bold witnesses, just like Jonah eventually was. Pray: 'Holy Spirit, fill me with Your power. Help me to hear Your voice clearly and give me the courage of a knight to obey.'"
    5. Prayer Ministry: Allow leaders to walk around and gently pray for children who have their hands raised or are responding to the Spirit. Pray for peace, bold obedience, and the baptism of the Holy Spirit.

    7. Closing Game / Activity (10 mins)

    Activity: "The Great Fish Relay"

    • Setup: Divide kids into two teams. Put a hula-hoop (representing the fish mouth) at the far end of the room for each team.

    • How to Play:

    • The first player on each team must run to the hula-hoop, crawl through it (representing being swallowed), do 3 jumping jacks inside the hoop (representing the 3 days in the fish), and then run back to tag the next player.

    • The team that completes the relay first wins!
    • Remind them: "Don't run away from the mission—run to finish it!"

    Closing Blessing: Mia (Lady Kindness) blesses the children, praying they will have a week filled with God's presence and hearing His voice.
